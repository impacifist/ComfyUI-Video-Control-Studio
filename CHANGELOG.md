# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Pose, Depth and Canny extraction from native ComfyUI VIDEO input.
- Synchronized A/B comparison, draggable divider, playback and frame stepping.
- Segment trimming, FPS conversion, resize and workflow presets.
- Local one-frame and segment execution without downstream generation.
- Clean IMAGE and silent VIDEO outputs.
- English and Korean interfaces, documentation and canvas workflows.
- Content-sized minimum node dimensions and stable playback timing.
